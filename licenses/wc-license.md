# The WorshipCommons License, Version 1.0

Songs in `songs/<lang>/wc-license/` folders are shared by their writers under
the WorshipCommons License: free for worship everywhere, forever; all commercial rights reserved
by the writer. The canonical, explained version lives at
https://worshipcommons.org/license — the license itself is these three
paragraphs:

> **1 · Worship Use** means performing, singing, or playing this song in the
> course of religious services or other gatherings held for worship — in a
> building, a home, online, anywhere — and everything done to prepare for and
> share those gatherings: rehearsing, projecting, printing, copying,
> distributing to the congregation, transposing, arranging, translating,
> recording, and streaming them, live or on demand.
>
> **2 · The grant.** Everyone taking part in or serving such worship is granted
> free, worldwide, non-exclusive, perpetual, and irrevocable permission to use
> this song — its words and music, together with the recordings and files
> shared with it — for Worship Use. This permission does not depend on the
> copyright law of any country; it applies identically everywhere.
>
> **3 · Everything else stays with the writer.** Any use that is not Worship
> Use — selling recordings or sheet music, sync licensing, broadcast, ticketed
> performances, rewriting the lyrics (translation aside) — needs the writer's
> permission.

Every song carries the license version in effect on the day it was shared.
Crediting the writer is appreciated, never required.

The commons is a gift economy: songs are shared in good faith and come **as is**,
without warranties. Whoever shares a song certifies they own it; churches that
relied on the license in good faith aren't the ones on the hook if that promise
fails.

*No WC-licensed songs are in the repo yet: the catalog's seeded songs are all
public domain, and writer-shared songs live in the WorshipCommons library at
worshipcommons.org. The layout and this license text exist so the licensing
story is complete for the day WC-licensed songs are exported here.*
