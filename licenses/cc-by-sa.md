# CC BY-SA — steward note

Songs under `songs/<lang>/cc-by-sa/` are released by their writers under the Creative
Commons Attribution-ShareAlike license. The version is per song: `song.json`
`licenseVersion` (`4.0` unless the source file says otherwise, e.g. `3.0`) and
`licenseUrl` (the exact license the writer applied). We never restamp a 3.0 file as 4.0.

**The legal code is the license.** We do not rewrite it. Read it at
<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode> (or the version named in the
song). The church-facing You may / You may not / You must deed lives in
[`licenses.json`](licenses.json) and is a summary; if it and the legal code disagree,
the legal code controls.

**What every copy must carry:** Credit as for CC BY, and share any translation or arrangement you distribute under CC BY-SA.

**Provenance.** Each song folder's `song.json` names the source (`provenance`,
`attribution`) and `sources.txt` is generated from it. A CC song with no
`licenseUrl` or attribution text fails validate.

**No warranty.** Determinations are made in good faith and are best-effort; if something
is here in error, tell us and we will review and remove it.
