# Public domain

Every song in a `songs/<lang>/public-domain/` folder — words, music, and the
files shipped with it — is public domain: free to use, copy, modify, perform,
record, and redistribute for any purpose, commercial included, with no
permission needed.

How that was established varies by source (pre-1931 US publication, per-file
copyright-line checks, or an explicit public-domain dedication by the source).
Each song folder's `sources.txt` and `song.json` (`provenance`) say exactly
where each file came from; the registry with license bases is
[`../sources.json`](../sources.json).

## Attribution

Attribution is not a legal condition of public-domain material, but one source
asks for credit when its files are reposted, and we pass that request on:

> Tune MIDIs marked `cyber-hymnal` in `sources.txt` come from **The Cyber
> Hymnal** (http://www.hymntime.com/tch/). If you republish them, please credit
> "The Cyber Hymnal" with a link.

A courtesy credit to the **Open Hymnal Project** (https://openhymnal.org) for
files marked `open-hymnal`, and to **Hymnary.org** for texts and the
hymnal-count metadata, is appreciated.

## No warranty

The public-domain status of every song here was verified in good faith — per-file
copyright-line checks, pre-1931 publication dates, or an explicit dedication by
the source — but this is a best-effort determination, not legal advice, and it
could contain errors. Copyright terms also differ by country: most of these
determinations follow US rules, and a work public domain in the US may still be
protected elsewhere. Everything is provided **as is**, with no warranty of any
kind — including accuracy of texts, tunes, metadata, and licensing status. You
are responsible for confirming a work's status for your use and jurisdiction.

If you believe something is included here in error, please tell us (an issue on
this repository or via worshipcommons.org) and we will review and remove it.
