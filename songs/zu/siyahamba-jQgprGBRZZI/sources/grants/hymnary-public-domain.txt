Source: https://hymnary.org/text/siyahamb_ekukhanyen_kwenkos

Hymnary.org text record: "Copyright: (c) 1984, Peace of Music Publishing AB, Walton Music Corp., a division of GIA Publications, Inc."; source "South African Traditional Song". The Zulu chorus was transcribed by Andries van Tonder in 1952 from oral tradition (authors unknown); the 1984 copyright covers Anders Nyberg's Freedom Is Coming edition. Our Verse 2 ("We are marching in the light of God") is the English line from that edition.
